Madeira Trail Slot Finder

Non-resident slot availability — Simplifica Madeira reservation system

Why this exists

Simplifica only shows availability one trail at a time with no overview — making it tedious to compare slots across trails and dates.

How it works
  • Fetches all trails at once and displays non-resident capacity as a color-coded heatmap
  • Set your group size to highlight slots that can fit your party
  • Exact capacity per slot is not published — percentage-to-headcount conversion is approximate (~40 assumed)
  • Slots below your threshold are marked ×
  • Data is cached locally for 1 hour
